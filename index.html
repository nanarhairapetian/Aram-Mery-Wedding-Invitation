Our Beginning

Our Love Story

[t started on a warm evening in Yerevan, somewhere between the pink tuff stone walls of Northern Avenue and the glowing lights of Republic Square. Neither Aram nor Mery was looking for anything out of the ordinary that day—just a quiet walk, a cup of coffee, and a break from the city's rush. Yet, one shared glance and an effortless conversation turned a passing moment into the beginning of everything. In Yerevan, love has a rhythm of its own. For Aram and Mery, it grew in the simplest places: late-night walks up the Cascade steps watching the city lights stretch toward Mount Ararat, sharing warm pastries from a corner bakery, and laughing over endless cups of Armenian coffee until the cafes closed. Aram brought a quiet strength and steady warmth to Mery’s world, while Mery filled Aram’s days with light, spontaneity, and a smile that made every ordinary street feel like a celebration.]

[Through changing seasons—from golden autumns along Saryan Street to crisp spring mornings in Lovers’ Park—what began as a spark deepened into a bond built on trust, patience, and genuine friendship. They learned each other’s silences as well as each other’s dreams, discovering that home wasn’t just an address in Yerevan, but wherever the two of them stood side by side.]
